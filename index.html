Dodge Party Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Dodge Party ("the game") is a mobile dodgeball game for Android. This page explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. In short: the game has no accounts, does not ask for your name, email or contacts, and keeps your progress only on your own phone. It shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some device information as described below.

Information kept on your phone

Your coins, level, unlocked items, the outfit and throws you choose, and your settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using Google AdMob: optional video ads you can choose to watch for a reward, and occasional full-screen ads between rounds. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • information about your device and how you interact with ads;
  • diagnostic information such as crash and performance data.

This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Playing with friends online

When you create or join a friend room, the game connects to our relay server so the phones in the room can talk to each other. It passes on the room code, the player name shown in the room, your outfit choice and the moves of the match, only between the phones in that room and only while you play. The relay does not store any of it, and your internet provider's normal connection details (such as your IP address) are used only to deliver the messages. If you never use friend rooms, nothing is sent.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Connections to the relay and to Google's ad services are encrypted in transit.
